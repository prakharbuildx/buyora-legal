5. Payment

  • Payment methods: orders are paid by Cash on Delivery or, where Buyora switches it on, by UPI prepayment ("Pay now"). Which methods you can use may depend on your delivery address: some areas may require prepayment, and Buyora may pause UPI prepayment at any time. The app shows the available methods before you place the order.
  • Cash on Delivery: you pay the delivery partner the exact order total when the order arrives (cash, or a payment method the delivery partner accepts at the door). Please keep the order total ready. The delivery partner records the payment in the app, and you get a notification.
  • How UPI prepayment works: after placing the order, you pay the exact order total to Buyora's merchant UPI ID from your own UPI app, then tap "I've paid". Buyora checks the payment manually in its merchant UPI app, using the amount, time and payer name or UPI ID/handle that app shows, and then confirms it. The shop receives your order only after Buyora has confirmed your payment.
  • Time to pay: you must pay within the time limit shown in the app for that order. If the time runs out before you have paid and tapped "I've paid", the order expires and is cancelled.
  • "I've paid" is not proof of payment. Tapping it asks Buyora to check; the order counts as paid only once Buyora confirms the payment. If we cannot find a matching payment, we may reject it and cancel the order. If you did pay, contact support with your order number and your UPI transaction reference.
  • The Buyora app never collects or stores card or bank details or UPI PINs. You enter your UPI PIN only in your own UPI app.

6. Cancellations and refunds

  • Cancelling by the customer: you can cancel an order in the app within 3 minutes and 30 seconds of placing it, while the shop has not yet started preparing it. A UPI prepaid order cannot be cancelled in the app while Buyora is checking its payment. After that, contact support; we will help where we can, but an order already being prepared or on its way may not be cancellable.
  • Rejection by the vendor: a shop or restaurant may reject an order before it is ready (for example, if an item is out of stock or they are closing), with a reason. You are told the reason in the app and by notification.
  • Cancellation by Buyora: we may cancel an order if it cannot be delivered, if there is a pricing or stock error, or if we suspect fraud or abuse.
  • Refunds, Cash on Delivery: because you pay at the door, nothing is charged for a cancelled or rejected order, so no refund is needed.
  • Refunds, UPI prepaid orders: if a prepaid order is rejected, cancelled or expires unpaid after you have in fact paid, Buyora refunds the amount you paid by UPI to the UPI account you paid from. Refunds are made manually by Buyora; the app shows when a refund is due and when it has been sent. If your order expired or was cancelled but you had already paid, contact support with your order number so we can match your payment and refund it.
  • Problems with an order: if you have paid for an order and there is a problem with it (missing, wrong or damaged items), contact support as soon as possible with your order number. We will look into it with the vendor and, where the complaint is valid, offer a fair solution, such as a replacement or a refund of the amount paid for the affected items.
