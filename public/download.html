How to install

  1. DownloadTap the orange button above. The file is called buyora-customer.apk.
  2. Open the fileWhen the download finishes, tap it in the notification, or find it in your Downloads / Files app.
  3. Allow "Install unknown apps"If Android asks, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for the app you downloaded with (Chrome, WhatsApp or your file manager). Then go back.
  4. InstallTap Install, then Open.

Seeing a warning? Android shows a warning for apps installed from outside the Play Store. This is normal. Buyora is downloaded straight from our official release page, and you can turn "Install unknown apps" off again after installing.
