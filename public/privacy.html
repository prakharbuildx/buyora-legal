Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short:

  • We collect only what we need to run orders and deliveries: your account details, delivery addresses, orders, and a notification token for your phone.
  • Payment is Cash on Delivery. We do not collect or store card, UPI or bank details.
  • The Buyora Driver app shares a delivery partner's location only while they are on an active delivery.
  • We do not sell your data. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and these web pages set no cookies.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

Buyora ("Buyora", "we", "us") runs a local grocery and food delivery service in Uttar Pradesh, India, through these apps:

  • Buyora: the customer app for ordering from local shops and restaurants;
  • Buyora Vendor: for shops and restaurants to manage their products and orders;
  • Buyora Driver: for delivery partners to pick up and deliver orders;
  • Buyora Admin: a web panel used only by Buyora staff to run the service.

For the purposes of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), Buyora is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described here. You can reach us at buyora103@gmail.com or +91 83032 50570 (phone and WhatsApp).

3. Who can see your data

We share only what each person needs for an order:

  • The vendor (shop or restaurant) sees your order: items, quantities, options, amounts, order number and delivery address.
  • The delivery partner sees the pickup address, your delivery address and map location, the order details and the amount to collect in cash.
  • Customers see the name and phone number of the delivery partner assigned to their order.
  • Buyora staff can see account, order and delivery data through the admin panel, to run the service, approve vendors and partners, and help with problems.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires it (for example, a valid order from a court, police or tax authority), or to protect the safety of people or prevent fraud.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account, addresses, cart, favourites, notification tokens: while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete them within 30 days (see Delete your account).
  • Orders and their payment records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require us to keep business records, and to deal with disputes or legal claims. After an account is deleted, we keep these records with your name, email, phone number and saved addresses removed wherever the law allows.
  • Delivery location: the latest position for each delivery, kept with that order's records.
  • Support conversations: only as long as needed to resolve the issue.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted database backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced.

6. How we protect your data

  • All app and website traffic is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database rules make sure each person can see only their own data, or only the orders they work on. Prices and order totals are calculated on our server, not in the app.
  • Admin access is limited to Buyora staff accounts.
  • Passwords are never stored in readable form.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens that affects you, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable Indian law you can:

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • Correct and update: change your name, phone number and addresses in the app, or ask us to correct anything else;
  • Delete: ask us to delete your account and personal data (how);
  • Withdraw consent: for example, turn off location or notification permission in your phone's settings, or stop using the service and delete your account. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was done before, and some features (such as delivery) cannot work without the data they need;
  • Nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or become unable to act;
  • Complain to our Grievance Officer (below), and, if you are not satisfied, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us from the email address or phone number registered on your account, so we can confirm it is you.
