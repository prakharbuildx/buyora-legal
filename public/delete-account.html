Delete your Buyora account

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Buyora, Buyora Vendor or Buyora Driver app, and what happens to your data.

1. How to ask

Send us a deletion request using the email address or mobile number registered on your account, so we can confirm the request comes from you:

WhatsApp: +91 83032 50570
Phone: +91 83032 50570
Email: buyora103@gmail.com (subject: "Delete my Buyora account")

Please include:

  • which app the account is for (Buyora, Buyora Vendor or Buyora Driver);
  • the name, email address and mobile number on the account.

We may ask you to confirm the request from your registered email address or phone number. We will not ask for your password.

2. Before we delete

  • Orders that are still in progress must be completed or cancelled first.
  • Vendors and delivery partners must first settle any cash collected or amounts due with Buyora.

3. What we delete

  • Your sign-in account (email and password), so you can no longer log in;
  • your name, mobile number and email address on your profile;
  • all saved delivery addresses and their map pins;
  • your cart and favourites;
  • notification tokens and your in-app notifications;
  • for vendors: your shop's listing is taken down; your products and photos are deleted unless the shop continues under another owner;
  • for delivery partners: your duty status and stored delivery locations linked to you, except as kept with order records below.

4. What we keep, and why

Indian tax and accounting laws require us to keep records of sales for several years, and we may need them to resolve disputes or legal claims. So we keep:

  • Order records: order number, date, items, prices, delivery fee, total, payment method and status, and the order's status history;
  • Cash on Delivery collection records for orders.

Before keeping them we remove your name, email address, phone number and saved addresses from your account, so these records are no longer linked to a person wherever the law allows. Records that the law requires to keep in full are kept only as long as the law requires and are then deleted.

Deleted data can stay in encrypted database backups for a limited time until those backups are replaced; it is not used during that time.

5. How long it takes

We confirm that we received your request, and complete the deletion within 30 days. We will tell you when it is done.

6. Other choices

  • To remove only some data, you can delete saved addresses, your cart and favourites in the app yourself, or ask us to remove specific data.
  • To stop notifications or location access, turn them off in your phone's settings for the app.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. Please send a request as above.

See our Privacy Policy for more about your rights.
